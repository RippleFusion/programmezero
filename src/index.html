About the show

A limited series from the people who live transformation.

Real stories, told in story arcs you can binge, by the people who sponsored, led and lived them.

Along the way we stop to explain the building blocks of transformation, so you know what you are watching.

  • HonestTold by the people who lived it.
  • Story-ledStory arcs, not lectures.
  • ExplainedEach element, clearly explained.

Meet the hosts

Ian Lloyd

Interim transformation director

Ian is an interim transformation director with experience in private equity backed and global businesses. He works alongside CEOs, boards and leadership teams, and takes accountability for the outcome.

Nadeem Lone

Founder & Director, Codesearch AI

Nadeem has spent 20 years in industry, from staffing at Adecco and Argyll Scott Technology to launching Codesearch AI in 2017. He has worked with brands from Google and AIG to fast-growing start-ups.

Paul Meredith

Fintech delivery leader

Paul is a delivery and change leader based in London. He has worked as a Delivery, Programme and Change Director in fintech, payments, banking and SaaS. Away from work, he helps run his local carnival and has led volunteers renovating an orphanage in Romania.

Carl Robinson

Chief Delivery & Transformation Officer

Carl is an interim Chief Delivery Officer and Chief Transformation Officer based in the UK. He helps organisations build the delivery and transformation capability they need to grow and change, working at board and executive level.

Questions

Who is Programme Zero for?

Anyone who sponsors, leads or delivers change, and the teams around them.

Who hosts it?

There are four of us: Ian Lloyd, Nadeem Lone, Paul Meredith and Carl Robinson. Not everyone joins every conversation, so each episode has its own line-up. Meet the team.

Where can I watch?

On YouTube, Spotify and Apple Podcasts. Use the Watch now button at the top of the page for the latest episode.

How often do new episodes come out?

Every week.

Can I be a guest?

Yes. If you would like to be a guest, use the Guests email on the contact page.

Get in touch

Whether you want to be a guest, work with us or talk to us about the show, we would love to hear from you.
