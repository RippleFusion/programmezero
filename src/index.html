About the podcast

Programme Zero goes back to where every programme starts.

Each episode follows one programme from the very beginning: the brief, the pressure and the first decisions. We talk to the people who sponsored, led and delivered it, and ask what worked, what didn't and what they would do differently.

  • HonestReal programmes, told by the people who were in the room.
  • PracticalLessons you can use on your own programme on Monday morning.
  • Jargon-freePlain conversation, no slide decks and no buzzwords.
